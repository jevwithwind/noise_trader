\section{Variable definitions}
\label{app:variables}
% =====================================================================

Notation follows Ohta (2026) where the quantity is his. A superscript $k \in
\{B, S\}$ denotes trades initiated by a buy or a sell market order; \emph{large}
means a trade of more than one trading unit and \emph{small} exactly one unit.
All prices are handled as integer tenths of a yen, so the digit arithmetic is
exact on every grid.

\subsection*{Clustering}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=6.5em, style=nextline, nosep, font=\normalfont\itshape]
\item[$\mathit{digit}$] The last price digit, $(p \bmod 10\tau)/\tau$ for tick
  size $\tau$. A \emph{round price} is one whose digit is zero. Under a uniform
  distribution each digit would carry 10\% of volume.
\item[$M^{0}$] Share of continuous-session traded volume executing at a round
  price.
\item[$M^{kLarge0}$] The same share within large trades initiated on side $k$.
  This is the paper's headline measure and the primary regressor here.
\item[$M^{kSmall0}$] The same within small trades. The mechanism predicts it
  behaves differently, and it serves as the internal contrast.
\item[$M^{d}$] Share of volume at last digit $d$, for $d = 0,\dots,9$. Digits
  $1$--$9$ are used only for the placebo test.
\item[$\mathit{Small}$] Share of volume in trades of exactly one trading unit.
\end{description}

\subsection*{Liquidity, as the paper defines it}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=6.5em, style=nextline, nosep, font=\normalfont\itshape]
\item[$\mathit{ES}$] Effective half-spread: the volume-weighted mean of
  $q(p - m)/m$ in basis points, where $m$ is the midquote prevailing immediately
  before the trade and $q$ is $+1$ for buy-initiated and $-1$ for sell-initiated
  trades.
\item[$\mathit{Imp}(\Delta)$] Price impact: the volume-weighted mean of
  $q(m_{t+\Delta} - m_t)/m_t$ in basis points, at $\Delta \in \{1\text{s},
  60\text{s}, 300\text{s}\}$. Trades whose horizon would run past the end of
  their own session are excluded for that horizon, so no impact is ever measured
  across the lunch break or the close.
\item[$\mathit{RS}(\Delta)$] Realized spread, $\mathit{ES} - \mathit{Imp}(\Delta)$,
  computed on that horizon's own trade set so the decomposition closes exactly.
\item[$\Delta\mathit{Imp}^{kLarge}$] The round-price impact premium: impact of
  large round-price trades minus impact of other large trades, on side $k$. Cells
  with fewer than five trades are set missing.
\item[$\mathit{Dep}^{\mathrm{ask}0}, \mathit{Dep}^{\mathrm{bid}0}$] Log mean depth
  at the best quote immediately before round-price trades, with the non-round
  counterparts $\mathit{Dep}^{\mathrm{ask}1}$, $\mathit{Dep}^{\mathrm{bid}1}$.
\item[$\mathit{QS}$] Time-weighted quoted spread in basis points, sampled on a
  one-minute grid over snapshots showing an ordinary two-sided book.
\item[$\mathit{Amihud}$] Absolute open-to-close return divided by turnover in
  millions of yen. Auxiliary only: it is included as a familiar yardstick, not as
  a substitute for the measures above.
\end{description}

\subsection*{Price formation}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=6.5em, style=nextline, nosep, font=\normalfont\itshape]
\item[$\mathit{RV}$] Realised variance of five-minute midquote log returns,
  computed within each session so no return spans the lunch break or the
  overnight gap.
\item[$\mathit{VR}$] Variance ratio, the variance of five-minute returns over
  five times the variance of one-minute returns. $|\mathit{VR} - 1|$ measures
  departure from a random walk in either direction.
\item[$\mathit{OFI}$] Order-flow imbalance at the best quote, following
  \textcite{ContKukanovStoikov2014}, accumulated over quote updates. Implemented
  from the published definition rather than taken from a library.
\end{description}

\subsection*{The visible ten-level book}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=6.5em, style=nextline, nosep, font=\normalfont\itshape]
\item[$\mathit{RDepth}^{\mathrm{ask}0}, \mathit{RDepth}^{\mathrm{bid}0}$] Share of
  visible ten-level resting volume sitting at round prices, time-averaged on a
  one-minute grid. This is the measure Ohta's data could not support: it observes
  round-price orders while they are still standing, rather than after they have
  been executed against. Under a uniform digit distribution it would be 10\%.
\item[$\mathit{Slope}$] Depth slope: the regression coefficient of level volume on
  level index, averaged across the two sides, after
  \textcite{NaesSkjeltorp2006}.
\item[$L^{k0}$] Round-price share of limit-order volume submitted on side $k$,
  inferred from ladder deltas within the visible book
  (Appendix~\ref{app:ladder}).
\item[$L^{k0C}$] Cancelled volume at round prices divided by submitted volume at
  round prices; $L^{k1C}$ is the same for other prices. The hypothesis is that
  round-price orders are cancelled \emph{less}, because they are executed against
  first.
\item[$L^{k0E}$] Executed volume at round prices divided by submitted volume
  there.
\item[$C^{k0}$] Round-price share of all cancelled limit-order volume.
\item[distance classes] Submissions are additionally split by where they sit
  relative to the prevailing best quote: inside it, at it, outside by less than
  0.5\%, and outside by 0.5\% or more.
\end{description}

\subsection*{Controls and sample variables}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=6.5em, style=nextline, nosep, font=\normalfont\itshape]
\item[$\tau$] Tick size for the stock-day, taken from the encoded JPX schedule and
  cross-checked against the finest increment observed on the tape.
\item[$\mathit{RelTick}$] Tick size divided by the opening price, in basis points.
\item[$D^{n} \times \mathit{Lowvol}$] Interaction of a dummy for the opening
  price's last digit being $n$ with a dummy for the previous day's realised
  variance falling below that day's cross-sectional median. These control the
  mechanical contamination documented in Ohta's Table 2 and reproduced in
  Section~\ref{sec:stylized}. Digit zero is the omitted category.
\item[$\mathit{ret}^{\mathrm{on}}$] Overnight return, previous close to opening
  price.
\item[$\mathit{Turnover}$] Traded value in yen; entered in logs.
\item[$\mathit{MktCap}$] Closing price times shares outstanding, from the daily
  summary product; entered in logs and used to form size quintiles.
\item[$\mathit{FineTick}$] Indicator for a stock-day on the 0.1-yen grid. These
  days enter the descriptive results and are excluded from the regressions,
  following the paper.
\end{description}
